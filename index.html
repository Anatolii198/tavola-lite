The kitchen

Twelve dishes. All of them finished at the pass, none of them waiting.

We cook what the market gives us that week, so the menu is short on purpose. Bread is baked every morning, pasta is rolled by hand, and the fire does most of the rest. If a dish is on the board, it is because it is worth the space.
